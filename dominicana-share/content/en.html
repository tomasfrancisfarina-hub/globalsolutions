01 · Executive summary

A clear, secured structure aligned with future growth.

The investor receives a defined return from the outset while retaining economic exposure to the asset’s future performance.

The capital is allocated to a hotel operation backed by real estate whose declared market value provides substantial coverage relative to the financed amount.

In addition to the agreed interest, the investor will retain an economic entitlement equal to 1% of the hotel’s annual net profitability and 1% of the net profit generated by a future sale of the asset, subject to the final contractual documentation.

06 · Additional economic participation

In addition to the fixed USD 600,000 return, the investor retains a 1% economic participation.

The participation applies to two separate sources and remains in place after repayment of the loan, according to the definition and duration established in the contracts.

1% of annual net profitability

The exact amount is calculated from the hotel’s documented net profitability.

Annual net profit× 1%Additional payment

Illustrative example: if annual net profitability were USD 1,000,000, the 1% participation would equal an additional USD 10,000 per year.

1% of net profit from a future sale

It is not calculated on the total sale price, but on the net profit as contractually defined.

USD 6 M net profit× 1%USD 60,000

Illustrative example: a sale at USD 15 M does not automatically imply USD 150,000. If the verifiable net profit were USD 6 M, the participation would be USD 60,000.
